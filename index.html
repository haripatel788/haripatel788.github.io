01 · Featured

MarketPulse

Forecasts how much a stock is likely to move over the next 1 to 30 trading days, with 80% and 95% price ranges. Each stock gets its own HAR volatility model fit on 10 years of prices. I backtested it on 112,700 predictions across 490 S&P 500 stocks. It beat an EWMA baseline on 86% of the 474 stocks I held out, and the actual close landed inside the 80% range 81% of the time. The backend is FastAPI with Supabase auth, rate limiting, and 27 integration tests.

Python · FastAPI · Pandas · Supabase

Co-founder · Since Apr 2026

Co-founding Novus.

I'm building Novus with Rishabh Teegavarapu and Anantheshwar Elumalai. We kept seeing the same problem: students at non-target schools don't have a clear path into investment banking or private equity. Wall Street Prep, WSO, and BIWS help, but they mostly assume you already have the connections. We want to help the students who don't.

Novus is an interview prep platform that universities set up for their students. I built the backend on Supabase, with a 40-table Postgres schema and row-level security so each school only sees its own data. I also built the grading. Llama 3.3 70B scores answers on technical accuracy, risk awareness, and communication, and on 37 of 40 test answers it came within 2 points of my own grades. I've written 80+ interview questions and gone over them with 8+ finance advisors, and we've talked with people at Deutsche Bank, JPMorgan, BlackRock, and Vanguard. The beta starts at Drexel's LeBow College of Business.
