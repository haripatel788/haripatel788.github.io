Featured project

MarketPulse

A forecast that answers a practical question: how much might this stock move?

474 held-out stocks scored

86–87% beat the baseline, depending on horizon

1–30 days forecast horizons

01

Start with uncertainty.

Instead of guessing whether a stock goes up or down, MarketPulse estimates the size of its likely move.

02

Make the range useful.

It translates volatility into clear 80% and 95% price ranges across the selected forecast horizon.

03

Test it honestly.

The model is scored on held-out stocks and compared with a simple baseline before its results are trusted.

MarketPulse fits a per-stock HAR volatility model using historical prices, then turns expected movement into calibrated 80% and 95% ranges. The held-out benchmark scored 474 stocks; the model beat its EWMA baseline on 86–87% of them depending on the forecast horizon.
