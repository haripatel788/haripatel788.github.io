MarketPulse

Stock volatility forecasting API. Per-stock Heterogeneous Autoregressive (HAR) models fit on 10 years of price history give the expected daily move and 80%/95% price ranges for up to 30 trading days. Backtested on 112,700 predictions across 490 S&P 500 stocks: it beat an EWMA baseline on 86% of 474 held-out stocks at 1-, 5-, and 20-day horizons, and its 80% ranges held the close 81% of the time. Supabase JWT auth, tiered access, sliding-window rate limits, and 27 pytest integration tests.

Co-Founder · Apr. 2026–Present

Novus — Fintech Startup.

I'm co-founding Novus with two partners, Rishabh Teegavarapu and Anantheshwar Elumalai. The idea came from a real gap: students at non-target schools have no structured way of breaking into high finance with roles like investment banking and private equity. The existing prep resources such as Wall Street Prep, WSO, BIWS, etc. are built for students who already have the network. Novus is built for everyone else.

Novus is a B2B platform for structured finance interview prep that universities run for their students. I architected the multi-tenant backend on Supabase: a 40-table PostgreSQL schema with Row-Level Security that keeps each institution's data isolated and enforces role-based access. I also built the AI grading pipeline, where Llama 3.3 70B scores answers on technical accuracy, risk awareness, and communication, landing within 2 points of expert graders 92.5% of the time. With 8+ finance industry advisors, including people at Deutsche Bank, JPMorgan, BlackRock, and Vanguard, we've written 80+ technical interview questions for the beta, starting at Drexel's LeBow College of Business.
